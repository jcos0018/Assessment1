Section 2: Research question

What is the trend in Australia’s population growth and median age over time? What is the relationship between median age and population growth across various countries?

Section 3: Data set introduction

The dataset used for this assignment is age and population data for selected countries, from 1950 to 2023. This dataset is a subset of a larger dataset (containing all countries, and more population and migration variables), available from the World Population Prospects 2024 website. The specific excel file is linked here..

Due to dataset restrictions for this assignment (1000 records), only 13 countries were selected for use in the final dataset. Australia, U.S.A., United Kingdom, plus the ten largest countries in the United Nations Economic and Social Commission for Asia and the Pacific (ESCAP). U.S.A. and United Kingdom were selected as comparable first world democracies to Australia, while the ESPAC countries were chosen due to being geographically in a similar economic region as Australia.

The final dataset includes 5 variables: Country name; Year; Population size; Population growth rate; and median age.

Section 7: Conclusion There is a strong negative relationship between the median age of a country, and its annual population growth. That is, on average, the higher the median age of a country, the lower its annual population growth is likely to be.

This conclusion is based on analysis of 2023 data from 13 countries: the ten largest in the Asia-Pacific region, plus Australia, United Kingdom, and United States of America. To strengthen this conclusion, further analysis could be conducted on a wider array of countries from various continents; and from a variety of different years.
