Section 2: Research question

What is the trend in Australia population growth and median age over time? What is the relationship between median age and population growth across various countries?

Section 3: Data set introduction

The dataset used for this assignment is age and population data for selected countries, from 1965 to 2023. This dataset is a subset of a larger dataset (containing all countries, and more population and migration variables), available here https://population.un.org/wpp/assets/Excel%20Files/1_Indicator%20(Standard)/EXCEL_FILES/1_General/WPP2024_GEN_F01_DEMOGRAPHIC_INDICATORS_COMPACT.xlsx.

The dataset used in this assigment includes 5 variables: Country name; Year; Population size; Population growth rate; and median age.

This chart shows the relationship (in 2023) between median age of a country, and its population growth, with size of dots indicating the population size of the country.

  • There is a negative relationship between median age and population growth rate of the selected countries.

Section 7 Conclusion There is a strong negative relationship between the median age of a country, and its annual population growth. That is, on average, the higher the median age of a country, the lower its annual population growth is likely to be.

This conclusion is based on analysis of 2023 data from 13 countries: the ten largest in the Asia-Pacific region, plus Australia, United Kingdom, and United States of America. To strengthen this conclusion, further analysis could be conducted on a wider array of countries from various continents; and from a variety of different years.
